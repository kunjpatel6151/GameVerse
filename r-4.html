About The Game


SYNCED is a futuristic free-to-play co-op shooter developed by NExT Studios and published by Level Infinite. In a world overrun by Nanos—rogue technology that has brought civilization to its knees—you will take on the role of a Runner, deploy with your squad or fight alone with your tamed Nano. Venture into the collapsed world for intense PvE and PvP combats and uncover the secrets buried deep inside the Meridian."Sync" the Nanos you defeat to control companion classes with different abilities: Crusher, Guardian, Seer, and Suppressor. Unleash your enemy-turned-ally companion on the battlefield to even the odds. Evolve your battle tactics over time with companion Nanos with customizable mods.Gear up as part of a 3-person team, utilizing squad tactics to out-maneuver the enemy and claim victory.Select the optimal Runner, Nano, and build to complement your squad. Formulate your team's real-time battlefield strategy by choosing from various weapons and loadouts.Each match plays out as a fresh run on non-linear battlefields. Take down Tyrant Nanos in progressively challenging PvE Dead Sectors. Pit your skills against rival Runners in high-octane PvP matches. Mod your Runner, weapons, and even your Companion Nanos in Each run to turn the tide of battle in your favor.
